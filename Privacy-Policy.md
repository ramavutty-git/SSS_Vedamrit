# Privacy Policy for SSS Vedamrit

**Effective Date:** May 31, 2026

SSS Vedamrit ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our Android application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

The App may store information that you voluntarily create within the App, including:

* Chanting progress and completion records
* Practice schedules and reminders
* Favorites, bookmarks, and preferences
* Notes created within the App

### Automatically Collected Information

To improve reliability and performance, the App may automatically collect:

* Device model and operating system version
* Application version
* Crash logs and diagnostic data
* Anonymous usage statistics
* Performance metrics

This information does not directly identify you.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App may use third-party services such as:

* Google Firebase Analytics
* Firebase Crashlytics
* Google Play Services

These services may collect device information and anonymous usage data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Audio Content

The App may stream or download Vedic chanting audio and spiritual content.

The App does not record, store, or transmit microphone audio unless a future feature explicitly requires microphone access and you grant permission.

## Permissions

Depending on enabled features, the App may request:

### Notifications

Used to send chanting reminders, practice schedules, and devotional notifications.

### Storage Access

Used to cache or download audio files and other content for offline access.

### Internet Access

Required to retrieve online content, updates, and audio resources.

Permissions are requested only when needed and may be revoked through Android settings.

## Advertising

If advertising is enabled in future versions, the App may display ads through advertising partners such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect identifiers and usage information to provide and measure advertisements in accordance with their privacy policies.

If no advertising is currently used, no advertising-related data is collected.

## Data Storage

Most user data, including chanting progress and preferences, is stored locally on your device.

If cloud backup or synchronization features are introduced in future releases, users will be informed and consent will be obtained where required.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be shared only:

* With service providers that help operate the App
* To comply with legal obligations
* To protect the rights, safety, or security of users and the App

## Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide App functionality and comply with legal obligations.

Locally stored data may be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing App data.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If such information is discovered, it will be deleted promptly.
